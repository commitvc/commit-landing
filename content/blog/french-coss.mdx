<p>France saw about the same number of open-source startups launched in 2020-2023 as during 2015-2020. I compiled a list of French COSS companies using Github, Dealroom, TechCrunch, and scraped data. Two main factors explain this acceleration: contextual and cultural.</p>

      <h2>Context: A startup nation under construction</h2>

      <ul>
        <li>1. The French educational system is adapting to meet the growing demand for tech professionals.</li>
        <li>2. A first generation of engineers gained experience at tech giants (OpenAI, Apple, Palantir) and are now starting their own companies.</li>
        <li>3. The French government has been supportive (BSPCE, French Tech, BPI non-dilutive financing), and increasing VC money is flowing in.</li>
        <li>4. As French startups become larger, they train new engineers, setting the stage for the next generation of founders.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Culture: Wine, Baguette, and Eiffel Tower</h2>

      <p>France has a long history of innovation and engineering, and a culture of creating high-quality systems that is well suited for open-source development.</p>

      <p>Open-source needs more than identifying a pain point — you need to solve it elegantly to resonate with the community.</p>

      <p>The founders are great engineers who experienced a problem firsthand, solved it smartly, and chose to share their solution with the world.</p>

      <p>Open-source is getting more popular thanks to successful entrepreneurs educating VCs on the model's potential.</p>

      <h2>In conclusion, I'm excited about the future of COSS startups in France</h2>

      <p>Three predictions:</p>

      <ul>
        <li>(i) Short-term: the rising trend is just starting, and more French companies will flourish.</li>
        <li>(ii) Middle-term: some companies from the list are among the most promising French startups. I expect at least 3 to become category leaders in 5-10 years.</li>
        <li>(iii) Long-term: what's happening with open-source will happen with deep-tech (quantum, photonics) in 10+ years.</li>
      </ul>
