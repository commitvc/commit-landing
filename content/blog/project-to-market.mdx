<p>Mid-June, I talked about open-source funding at OW2Con. While there's a lot to discuss, one of my favorite aspect is the flywheel that kicks in when you align your project, community, product, and market. Some attendees requested the visuals I used, so I'm sharing them here with added context :)</p>

      <h2>(i) the Project</h2>

      <p>Most COSS journeys start with an open-source project. Some founders build a product from day one, others start with a side project that gains traction.</p>

      <h2>(ii) the Community</h2>

      <p>Once public, the project might attract users forming a community. Ideally, users contribute to the project, increasing its value.</p>

      <h2>(iii) the Product</h2>

      <p>As the project matures, a commercial product takes shape. Should bring superior value or ease adoption vs the project. Should complete the project, strengthening its openness — see project-product fit.</p>

      <h2>(iv) the Market</h2>

      <p>The product addresses a market that pays: solve a pain, get paid (nihil sub sole novum).</p>

      <h2>(v) the Flywheel</h2>

      <p>It should become seamless for qualified users from the community to become customers... and for customers to join the community, enriching its diversity and expanding contributors' base.</p>
